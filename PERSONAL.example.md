# Personal layer (example)

Copy to `PERSONAL.md` (gitignored) and make it yours. It overrides the Defaults in `AGENTS.md`, never the Locked Rules. Preferences and how you want agents to work only: no personal facts, since every session loads this file.

## Places

- Private notes (personal facts, parked tangents): `~/notes/`
- Vault (plans, red-team reports, comprehension debt): `~/docs-vault/<repo-name>/`

## Show it visually first

On any deliverable I'll review (spec, summary, plan, step guide), show its shape first: a Mermaid diagram, hierarchy or table, then the prose.

## Writing style

- Be direct. Skip preamble.
- No sycophantic validation ("great catch", "excellent point"). State the finding's disposition (confirmed, fixed, rejected) and move on.
- No filler counts ("12 files, 3 new hooks"). Numbers stay only when they drive a decision.
- Show artifacts and diffs rather than explaining what was done.
