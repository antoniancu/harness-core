# Personal layer (example)

Copy to `PERSONAL.md` and make it yours. It overrides the Defaults in `AGENTS.md`, never the Locked Rules. Preferences and how you want agents to work only: no personal facts, since every session loads this file.

## Show it visually first

On any deliverable I'll review (spec, summary, plan, step guide), show its shape first: a Mermaid diagram, hierarchy or table, then the prose.

## Writing style

- Be direct. Skip preamble.
- No sycophantic validation ("great catch", "excellent point"). State the finding's disposition (confirmed, fixed, rejected) and move on.
- No em dashes in anything I'd read or publish. Use `-`, a period or parentheses; for arrows use `->`.
- Run the `unslop` skill's checklist over any prose before handing it over. Silently.
- No filler counts ("19 migrations, 2 new crons"). Numbers stay only when they drive a decision.
- Show artifacts and diffs rather than explaining what was done.
